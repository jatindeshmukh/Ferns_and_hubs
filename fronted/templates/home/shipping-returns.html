Shipping & Returns

We ship across India. Orders are usually processed within 1–3 business days. Delivery timelines may vary based on your location and chosen shipping option.

Estimated Delivery:
  • Metro Cities: 3–5 working days
  • Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities: 5–7 working days
  • Remote Locations: 7–10 working days

  • Free Shipping on all orders above ₹999.
  • For orders below ₹999, a standard shipping fee of ₹49–₹99 applies depending on the location.

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive an email and SMS notification with a tracking link to monitor the progress of your delivery in real time.

We accept returns for damaged, defective, or incorrect items within 7 days of delivery.

Conditions for Returns:
  • Items must be unused and in original packaging.
  • Proof of purchase (order ID/invoice) is required.
  • Certain items like live plants may not be eligible for return unless damaged during transit.

Refunds are processed within 5–7 working days after approval. The refund will be credited to your original payment method (bank account, card, or wallet).

Exchanges are allowed for defective or incorrect products, subject to availability. Please contact our support team to initiate the exchange process.
