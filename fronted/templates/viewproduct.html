About the Product

The Jade Plant Mini is a charming, compact succulent with thick, glossy leaves that store water. Known as a symbol of good luck and prosperity, this easy-to-care-for plant is perfect for beginners and makes an excellent addition to any indoor space. Its low maintenance nature and air purifying qualities make it a favorite among plant enthusiasts.

Why Choose Jade Plant Mini?

Perfect Gift

Care Instructions

Light Requirements

Bright, indirect sunlight. Avoid direct harsh sun which can scorch the leaves.

Watering

Water when soil is completely dry. Avoid overwatering as it can cause root rot.

Temperature

Ideal between 15°C to 24°C. Protect from frost and extreme temperature changes.

Humidity

Normal room humidity. No special requirements, making it perfect for indoor environments.
